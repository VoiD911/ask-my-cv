1. **Statistics:**
   - Counts get +0.5 smoothing on both sides.
   - Scores are binned in 0.1 steps below the 0.5 threshold, with pairs of bins merged under 100 scores.
   - The alert level is the 99th percentile of the drift measure (PSI) under the null hypothesis: 5,000 draws of n scores from the baseline, fixed seed. A warning fires above the 95th percentile.
   - The threshold must fall on a bin edge, so 0.55 is now rejected with an error.

   The test that a sample drawn from the reference itself doesn't alert passes at n = 30, 60, 150 and 400 (300 fixed seeds each, at most 1% alerts). On 1,000 fresh seeds the alert rates were:

   | n | alert rate |
   |---|---|
   | 30 | 1.2% |
   | 60 | 1.0% |
   | 150 | 0.7% |
   | 400 | 1.0% |

   The 1.2% at n = 30 is within sampling noise of 1%.

2. **Reference:** each population (questions and ads) is compared with itself over time: the last 7 days against the previous 28 days, same model version. The eval-set histograms in `metrics.json` now only produce a warning, never a failure. Nothing is stored between runs.

3. **Explicit failures:** the job now fails, so the `report` job opens an issue, when:
   - there are no `injection` spans at all in the period;
   - the promoted version has no spans while other versions do;
   - the query hits its 10,000-row limit;
   - a population stays under 30 usable scores over the last 14 days while the version has been in service for at least 14 days. This comes from querying a 14-day window, not from stored state.

   Otherwise, fewer than 30 scores over 7 days is still "données insuffisantes": the job succeeds and adds a note to the job summary.
4. **Settings and parsing:** the ad cutoff comes from `guardrail_min_chars` in `settings.aws.yaml`, falling back to the `Settings` default. `xops.chars` is accepted as a string or a number, with tests for both and for invalid values.
5. **Minors:** there is a test that a malformed version passed to `main()` fails before any AWS call. The test that froze the old epsilon behaviour is removed.

Two things to watch after deploy:
- **Ad traffic:** ads are rare on the site. If they stay under 30 scores over 14 days, the job will fail on that rule every night, as the review asked. You would then either accept those alerts or lengthen the window.
- **`xops.chars` format:** I haven't seen it on a real span. It should be flattened the same way as `xops.score` in `aws/spans`, but I haven't confirmed it.
